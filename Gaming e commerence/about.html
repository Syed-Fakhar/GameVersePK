Our Story

PAKISTAN'S ULTIMATE
HARDWARE HAVEN

GameVerse PK was established in 2021 in Lahore, founded by a collective of competitive esports players and custom hardware rig designers who recognized a gap in the local market. Gamers were struggling to source authentic high-end items, often facing exaggerated markups, counterfeit products, or zero warranty options.

We set out to change the status quo. By establishing direct relationships with global manufacturers and official regional distributors (such as Sony, ASUS ROG, Razer, and Logitech), we introduced a transparent pricing system and 100% authentic inventory to Pakistani gaming communities.

Our Mission

To empower casual and professional gamers in Pakistan by offering instant access to original, high-performance equipment. We strive to provide competitive pricing, reliable warranty terms, and enthusiast-level technical support.

Our Vision

To serve as the cornerstone of the Pakistani esports infrastructure, helping players compete globally by providing access to world-class setups, building dynamic gaming communities, and hosting nation-wide tournaments.

Gaming Community Focus

We are more than just a store. We actively invest back into local communities to grow the competitive gaming landscape in Pakistan.
